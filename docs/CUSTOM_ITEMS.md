# New Custom Items

The following items were created to fulfill the requirements for the "Tools of the Trade" equipment kits and have been added to `item-data/index.ts`.

## General & Agency Gear

-   **Agency badge and identification card**: Flavor item with no game mechanics.
-   **Encrypted laptop**: A more secure version of a standard computer.
-   **Small evidence-collection kit**: Provides a bonus to Forensics checks.
-   **Windbreaker jacket (agency printed)**: Flavor item, clothing.
-   **Vest with agency identification**: Flavor item, clothing.
-   **Duty belt**: Flavor item, clothing.
-   **Pen and pocket notebook**: Flavor item.
-   **DSLR Camera**: High-quality camera for evidence gathering.
-   **Lab Coat**: Standard protective clothing for scientific work.
-   **Discreet Civilian Clothing**: Nondescript attire for blending in.

## Tactical & Protective Gear

-   **Battering ram**: A melee weapon used for breaching doors.
-   **Ballistic shield**: A form of portable cover that provides an Armor Rating.
-   **Knee and elbow pads**: Flavor item, clothing.
-   **Gloves**: Flavor item, clothing.
-   **Fire-retardant balaclava**: Flavor item, clothing.
-   **Tinted goggles**: Flavor item, clothing.
-   **Assault webbing**: Flavor item, clothing.
-   **Dog tags**: Flavor item.
-   **Combat Uniform**: Standard-issue military attire.
-   **Rucksack**: Large military-style backpack.

## Weapons & Explosives

-   **Smoke grenade**: A non-damaging grenade used for concealment.

## Survival & Tools

-   **Hydration system (e.g., Camelbak)**: A wearable hydration pack.
-   **Rappelling harnesses and equipment**: Gear for climbing and rappelling.
-   **Multi-tool**: A versatile tool providing a bonus to certain Craft checks.
-   **Compass**: A standalone navigation tool.
-   **MREs (Meals, Ready-to-Eat)**: Standard military field rations.
-   **Entrenching Tool**: Collapsible military shovel.
-   **Poncho and Liner**: Versatile military-issue waterproof gear.

## Medical & Scientific

-   **Hemostatic gel**: An advanced first aid item for stopping bleeding.
-   **Self-applying tourniquet**: A medical item for treating severe limb wounds.
-   **Geiger Counter**: Detects radiation.
-   **Water and Soil Sampler**: For collecting environmental evidence.
-   **Decontamination Kit**: For neutralizing hazardous materials.
-   **Scalpel Set**: For precision cutting or field surgery.
-   **Syringes**: For injections or sample collection.

## Covert & Intelligence

-   **Covert Comm Device**: A nearly invisible earpiece/microphone.
-   **Untraceable Funds**: Cash or crypto with no paper trail.
-   **Covert Surveillance Kit**: Miniature cameras and microphones.
-   **Escape and Evasion Kit**: Concealable tools for escaping captivity.
